Welcome

OCYM Kollam Diocese

The Orthodox Christian Youth Movement (OCYM) is the active youth wing of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church. Through faith, fellowship, and service, we seek to bring young people together to develop leadership skills and deepen their spiritual life.

🙏

Worship

Deepening our spiritual lives through liturgy, regular prayer meetings, devotional songs, and local parish retreats.

📖

Study

Reflecting on Holy Scripture, Church tradition, theology, and contemporary challenges faced by young Christian believers.

🤝

Service

Serving our community through active blood donation campaigns, education funding, medical aid, and parish outreach projects.
