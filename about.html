About OCYM Kollam Diocese

The Orthodox Christian Youth Movement (OCYM) of Kollam Diocese functions as the youth wing of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church.

Worship • Study • Service
✝

Spiritual Fellowship & Commitment

OCYM Kollam actively coordinates prayer fellowships, leadership courses, charitable drives, cultural programs, and youth retreats that strengthen unity and Christian values across 64 parishes in the Kollam Diocese.

Our Vision

To lead young people to Christ, encourage active involvement in the Orthodox Church, and nurture responsible Christian leaders.

🎯

Our Mission

To develop spiritually strong, socially committed, and leadership-oriented youth through programs that align with Orthodox traditions.

📈

Years of Service

Proudly serving the Kollam Diocese for over 60 years, coordinating retreats, Kalamela celebrations, blood banks, and outreach initiatives.
